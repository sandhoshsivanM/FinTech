Why this is different

Most finance apps want your bank login. Not your transactions — your login.

Because the business isn't the app. The business is the aggregated data behind it, and you are the raw material. Khazana takes the opposite approach: your records live in an encrypted database on your own device, unlocked by a PIN only you know. We cannot read them. There is no password reset either, because a recovery path we controlled would mean we could open your vault.

Encrypted at rest

AES-256 on every platform, keyed by PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 at 600,000 iterations. The key is derived from your PIN and never stored.

No server, by design

No account to create, no data to breach, nothing that stops working when someone stops paying a hosting bill.

Real accounting

Double-entry postings where every journal entry sums to zero — so net worth is derived, not guessed.

A portfolio that computes

Lot-level FIFO matching, XIRR, sector-wise profit and loss, and capital-gains estimation under rules versioned by disposal date.

Money that is exact

Arbitrary-precision decimal end to end, stored as text. Totals never drift by a rounding cent.

Two apps, one engine

Mobile and web compute identical numbers — enforced by tests that fail if the two implementations ever disagree.

macOS will warn you the first time you open it — here's why, and what to do

This build is not signed with an Apple Developer certificate, so Gatekeeper will say Khazana “cannot be opened because the developer cannot be verified”. That is macOS reporting a fact about the certificate, not a problem it has found with the app.

To open it: drag Khazana to Applications, then right-click (or Control-click) the app and choose Open, and confirm. You only need to do this once.

We would rather tell you this in advance than have it surprise you. A signed and notarised build needs a paid Apple Developer account, and that is on the list before Khazana is sold as a desktop download.

Two things we will never do

We will never cap your ledger. A capped ledger is a broken ledger, so there are no limits on what you can record, in any tier, ever. And we will never hold your data hostage. Encrypted backup, restore, a full CSV dump and "erase all data" are free forever — nothing that gets your own data out of Khazana will ever sit behind a payment. Both of these are enforced by our test suite, not just promised on a web page.

Questions people actually ask

If there's no server, what happens if I lose my phone?

You restore from your own encrypted backup. Khazana can export an encrypted backup file that only your PIN opens — keep it wherever you keep important files. Backup and restore are free forever, on both apps.

What if I forget my PIN?

Your data is gone, and we cannot recover it. That is not a gap — it is the design. Your PIN derives the encryption key, the key is never stored, and any recovery path we controlled would mean we could open your vault. The guarantee is only real if it is inconvenient for us too.

Do you see any of my financial data?

No, and not as a policy we could quietly change — there is nowhere for it to go. The web app makes zero requests. The mobile app calls price and currency providers only when you ask it to refresh, carrying ticker symbols and currency codes, never anything about you. There is no telemetry and no analytics.

Is Pro really a one-time payment?

Yes. One payment unlocks Pro and you keep it. There is no subscription, no renewal and no tier that expires. On the App Store it is a Universal Purchase with Family Sharing, so one buy covers your iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Does my purchase carry across the app stores and the website?

Not automatically, and we would rather say so before you pay than after. An app-store purchase and a direct licence key are separate, because linking them would require an account server — and we do not have one. Buy on the platform you use most.

Can I move my data out?

Any time, in a format that needs no Khazana to read. Full CSV export is free and always will be. Leaving must always be possible.
